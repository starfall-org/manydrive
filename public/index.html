Unified File Management

Browse My Drive, shared folders, and files across every connected provider from one clean, Material 3 interface.

🔐

Device-Bound Encryption

Access keys and imported private keys are encrypted with AES-GCM using Android Keystore — keys never leave your device.

🖼️

Google Photos Upload

Upload images, videos, and folders straight to Google Photos — with automatic album creation for folders.

🔑

Google Account Picker

Select an account already on your device and grant Drive access directly — only your selected email is persisted.

☁️

Streamed Transfers

Uploads and downloads stream content directly through the official SDKs, using temporary files that are cleaned up after each transfer.

🔍

Shared Resources

Access incoming shared drives and files by parent ID, including children accessible through inherited permissions.

Connect Your Providers

Add as many accounts as you need — the active provider is restored on restart.

🟦 Google Drive

Connect any Google account on the device. Full My Drive and Shared access, plus Google Photos uploads.

🟨 Amazon S3

Add any HTTPS S3-compatible endpoint, bucket, region, and access keys. Multiple connections supported.

🧾 Google Service Account

Import a service-account JSON key, obtain an OAuth token, and browse its Drive — validated before saving.

Your Data, Your Choice

We built ManyDrive around a simple principle: your files are yours. The app operates on your device, and your connected credentials are encrypted and stored on your own device.
