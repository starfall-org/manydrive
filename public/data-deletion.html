Data Deletion Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

ManyDrive is committed to protecting your privacy and honoring your right to control your data. This page explains how your data is stored and exactly how you can request deletion of your data and accounts.

What Data Does ManyDrive Store?

ManyDrive does not operate servers that store your personal data or files. All account data that the App saves is stored locally on your own device, in the App's private directory, encrypted with AES-GCM using Android Keystore keys. This data consists of:

  • The email address of the Google account you selected;
  • Your S3 connection details (endpoint, bucket, region, access keys);
  • Your imported Google Service Account JSON key (private key).

Your files and their content are not copied to us — they remain in the cloud providers (Google Drive, Amazon S3, etc.) that you connected. Access tokens are held only in memory during a session.

How to Delete Your Data

You can delete your data in either of the following ways. Both are effective immediately.

1

Delete an account inside the App

Open the profile / account menu, select an account, and choose "Remove". This immediately deletes that account's saved credentials (for Google, S3, or Service Account) from your device. Removing an account does not delete your remote files from the connected cloud provider.

2

Clear all app data or uninstall

In Android: Settings → Apps → ManyDrive → Storage → Clear data, or simply uninstall the App. Either action permanently removes every locally saved credential, setting, and connection from your device.

3

Request deletion from the developer

Email nptvscode@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and include the email associated with the account you want removed. Because we do not maintain servers storing your personal data, complying primarily addresses any data you may have sent to us directly (for example, support correspondence). We will confirm the deletion and respond within 30 days.

Note about your connected cloud accounts: the App only deletes the connection credentials stored on your device. It does not and cannot delete files inside your Google Drive or S3 buckets, nor your Google accounts themselves. To permanently delete your stored files or entire accounts on those services, please use the deletion tools provided by those services or remove the App's access in your Google account security settings.

Deleting the Active Google Session

Use Sign out in the App to clear the active Google session. You can also revoke the App's access directly at any time in your Google account settings (see Third-party apps with account access). Revoking access stops the App from using your Google account data.

How Long We Keep Data

No personal data is retained by the developer. Data stored locally on your device is kept only until you delete it (as described above), clear app data, or uninstall the App. We do not keep backups of your locally stored credentials.

Questions?

If you have any question about deleting your data, contact us at nptvscode@gmail.com and we will be happy to help.
