Short version: ManyDrive is designed to run almost entirely on your device. It does not sell or rent your personal information, does not show advertising, and does not build advertising profiles. The files you manage stay in your connected accounts (Google Drive, S3, service accounts); ManyDrive does not store your files on our servers. You can request full deletion of account data at any time.

1. Introduction

ManyDrive ("the App", "we", "us") is developed and operated by ManyDrive (Starfall), reachable at nptvscode@gmail.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled in connection with your use of the App, how it is used, and the choices and rights you have — including your right to request deletion of your data.

By using the App, you agree to the handling described in this policy. Because the majority of your data is stored by the storage providers you choose to connect (for example Google Drive), this policy focuses on what the App itself does with your information.

2. Information We Collect

The App is designed so that it does not need a personal account of its own. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, payment information, or other personal identifiers on our servers. What is handled is described below.

Data Type Collected? Purpose
Your selected Google account email address Personal / account identifier Yes — stored on your device only To remember which account you connected so the app can restore your active provider on restart. It is never sent to our servers (see section 3).
OAuth access tokens Authentication credential Yes — held in memory, not written to disk To authenticate requests to Google Drive / Photos on your behalf.
S3 endpoints, buckets, region, and access keys you provide Cloud credentials Yes — stored encrypted on your device only To browse and transfer files on the S3-compatible endpoint you specify.
Google Service Account JSON key you import Cloud credential (private key) Yes — stored encrypted on your device only To authenticate and access the Drive of that service account.
Files and metadata (names, sizes, dates, folder structure) User files (your content) No — accessed via your own accounts, not stored by us To list, browse, download and upload your own files through the official SDKs.
Device identifiers, analytics, advertising IDs Technical No We do not collect device identifiers and do not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

Personal and Sensitive Data: The App does not require or process sensitive data such as race, religion, health, sexual orientation, financial account numbers (beyond cloud provider fields you voluntarily add), or precise real-time location. Data handled in the screens above is only used for the core service you request.

3. Device-Only Data & Encryption

All credentials we save (your selected Google account email, S3 connection details, imported Service Account keys) are stored only in the App's private, backup-excluded directory on your device. They are encrypted at rest with AES-GCM using device-bound keys provided by the Android Keystore. These keys never leave your device.

  • OAuth access tokens are kept only in volatile memory for the duration of a session and are not written to disk.
  • The JSON service-account source file you import is read once; its file path is not retained.
  • Signing out clears the active session. Removing an account deletes its saved connection from your device without deleting any remote files.
  • Reinstalling the App, clearing its data, or moving to another device erases all locally stored credentials, so accounts must be reconnected.

4. Permissions & Third-Party Services

ManyDrive connects to third-party services you explicitly authorize. Each of these services has its own privacy policy and terms, and their data handling is governed by them, not by ManyDrive.

  • Google Drive Service — File access is provided through Google's official Drive SDK using the scope you grant. We request access only to your Google Drive and Photos Library to provide the file-management features you use. For privacy information, see Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Amazon S3 / S3-compatible endpoints — you provide the endpoint, buckets, region, and access keys. Content is transferred directly between your device and that endpoint.
  • Google Service Account — a JSON key is imported and used to obtain an OAuth token to browse that account's Drive.

We request your Google account access only for the account you pick. Only the selected account's email is persisted on your device.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Because the App does not run its own servers for your content, there is no personal data for us to share. We do not disclose personal data to third parties except:

  • When you directly connect to a service you chose (for example, delivering a file to the S3 endpoint or Google account you specified); or
  • When required by applicable law, regulation, or legal process.

6. Data Retention & Security

Your file content lives in the accounts you connect to, so its retention is controlled by those services and by your own actions. Credentials stored on your device are removed when you remove an account, sign out, clear app data, or reinstall the App. We do not retain copies of your files on our side.

We apply industry-standard security measures, including AES-GCM encryption via Android Keystore, secure in-memory token handling, and SDK-streamed transfers that use temporary files cleaned up after each transfer.

7. Your Choices & Data Deletion

You control your data. Here is how you can request deletion of your data:

  • Delete locally within the App: open the account menu and remove any Google, S3, or Service Account connection. This deletes the encrypted credentials from your device.
  • Sign out: the active Google session is cleared without removing accounts from the device. You can also revoke access at any time in your Google account settings.
  • Clear app data / reinstall: completely erases all locally saved credentials and settings.
  • Request deletion from us: email nptvscode@gmail.com. Because we do not operate servers storing your personal data, a request primarily addresses the minimal data you may have sent us (for example, support correspondence). We act on reasonable deletion requests.

For the full description of our deletion process and how to submit a request, see our Data Deletion Policy.

8. Children's Privacy

ManyDrive is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age of digital consent in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app or legal requirements. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will notify you within the App.
